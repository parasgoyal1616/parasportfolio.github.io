I'm Paras Goyal, a pharmacy undergraduate at the University Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences, Panjab University, Chandigarh, with a strong inclination towards graphic design, visual communication and digital media.

My creative journey involves designing social media campaigns, institutional promotional materials, scientific posters, brochures and event graphics. Through my involvement in student organisations and creative teams, I've explored how design can build identity, communicate ideas and create meaningful engagement.

Alongside graphic design, I enjoy video editing and digital content creation, bringing together visuals, motion and storytelling.
